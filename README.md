# +P (and some reduced-recoil) loads
## Check this shit out
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CiarenceW/PlusPRounds/refs/heads/master/cool_saa_image.png)  

## Features
### Bullets:
#### This mod adds +P rounds, with all the bells and whistles:  
- Increased bullet mass
- Increased round speed
- Increased recoil
- Increased malfunctions  
<b>More effective, but harder to control.</b>

#### On top of that, there's also some Reduced-Recoil rounds, just for fun, here's what they do, woah:  
- Decreased recoil  
- Decreased bullet mass  
- Decreased speed  
- Increased malfunctions  
<b>Less effective, but follow up shots are easier, especially for the Deagle :)</b>

<b>All those rounds have custom textures for the ammo boxes in the Compound.</b>

### Lore stuff:  
### Three tapes:  
- Bear load (in the SAA tape group)  
- FBI load (in the revolver tape group)  
- Overpressure (in the standard group)  
<b>These have an increased chance of spawning if you haven't collected them, but once you have, they become as rare (or common) as any other tape.</b>

### Two help entries:  
- Overpressure (unlocked the first time you pick up a +P round)  
- Underpressure (unlocked the first time you pick up a reduced-recoil or match or competition or underpressure or -p round)  

(No AI used, aren't I special?)
## HOW 2 USE  
### Install  
Just put the folder in your plugins directory, or don't do anything if you're using a mod manager, gulp  

### In game  
They have a chance to spawn in the Dreaming, and everywhere else.  
Oh and there's a config thing to disable it in Classic mode.  

#### When the bullet inventory is shown:  
- Press X (by default, Modding Kit keybind) to drop the selected round.
- Press Left/Right Arrow to switch selected rounds.  
  
#### In the Compound:  
- Go up to an ammo box and press Left/Right arrow to switch the current ammo in da box.
